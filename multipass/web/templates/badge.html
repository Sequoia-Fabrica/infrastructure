A portrait 54×85.6 mm card, laid out for the xTool P2. Each color is its own layer in xTool Creative Space:

  • blue guilloche: score, lowest power that marks
  • green lettering: score
  • black QR code and PDF417: fill engrave
  • red outline and slot: cut, last
  • grey (back only): outline for lining up; don't output

For the back, flip the cut card over in the hole it left in the stock, left to right, and engrave the back file in the same place.

The back's barcode is the kind ID scanners read, signed by {{.makerspace_name}} rather than a state. It shows your name and member number, and no date of birth.
